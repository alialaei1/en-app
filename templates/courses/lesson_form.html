STUDY SEGMENTATION (very important)
========================================
Goal: create pieces that are comfortable for studying — not too long, not too short.

Target length per piece:
- Prefer about 8–18 spoken words.
- Ideal: one clear idea / one breath group.
- Avoid tiny fragments of 1–4 words unless the speaker truly pauses there as a standalone line.
- Avoid long pieces over ~22–25 words. If a sentence is long, split it into natural spoken chunks.

How to split:
- Split on natural spoken pauses, commas that mark a breath, “and/but/so/because”, and clause boundaries.
- Keep tightly connected phrases together (e.g. “of course”, “I think”, “as always”).
- A period does NOT automatically force a new piece. Use blank lines to define pieces.

Questions:
- Keep a full question in one piece when it is short/medium.
- If a question is long, split into: setup/context piece(s) + the actual question piece.
- Never separate a question word from its core (Who/What/Where/When/Why/How + the key verb phrase) across awkward cuts.
- Tag question pieces with a highlight on the question word when useful, e.g. [[Why|چرا]] / [[How|چطور]].
- Put the meaning of the question on @= for that same piece.
